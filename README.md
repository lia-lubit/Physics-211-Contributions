# Physics-211-Contributions
Contributions to the Physics 211 course

This folder contains new lecture notes for the physics 211 github. I study cosmology, and wanted to look into the connections between the topics in our class (partifcle distributions, moments of functions, etc.) and cosmology. I wrote up these notes in the hopes that future 211 students will find them interesting and helpful, and that they may serve as a motivation for the study of stat mech for cosmologically-inclined students. I know that class time is limited, but I this topic could be interesting as a brief aside, or as a supplment to the Github for students who want to look beyond the standard course material. I hope these are helpful!
